---
file: 05-meta-info-anywhere.txt
brief: Test mmd with meta info block.
details: A MetaInfo block can be anywhere and is not rendered. A block of its own gives no <s-paragraph>; a block inside text splits the <p> but stays invisible. A blank line inside a block belongs to the block; only the closing `---` ends it.
note: The text contains excerpts from "Herman Melville, *Moby-Dick* (1851)".
---

# Ship's log

---
ship: Pequod
captain: Ahab
---

The whale is white.
---
note: this line is not shown
---
The sea is wide & deep, and 3 < 4 > 2.
