# Em dash

## In a paragraph

In a village of La Mancha -- the name of which I have no desire to call to mind -- there lived a gentleman.
He was of a hardy habit---spare, gaunt-featured---a very early riser and a great sportsman.

## In a list

The mates of the Pequod:
- Starbuck -- the chief mate,
- Stubb---the second mate,
  - with his pipe -- even in a storm.

## In a heading -- like this one

The heading above has a spaced em dash.

## Not an em dash

Run `ls --all` to see the hidden files, and read pages 1--2.
``` text
a---b -- c
```
