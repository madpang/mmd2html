details: Fuzzy headings take their level from the explicit heading above. `===` is one level below, `---` is two levels below, or one level below a `===`.
note: The text contains excerpts from "Liu Cixin, *The Three-Body Problem* (2008)".

## The Three-Body Problem

=== Red Coast

A signal goes out to the stars.

--- The answer

Do not answer!

=== Three Body

--- The game

A sun rises, then three.
